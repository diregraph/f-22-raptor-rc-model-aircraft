How it works

Bearing 1 is square to the duct. Bearings 2 and 3 are canted 40° in opposite directions. Turning 2 and 3 by equal and opposite angles bends the jet; bearing 1 turns the whole bend so it points straight down, and a little more or less to swing the jet sideways for yaw. Nothing rides on the nozzle but cords (yellow). Two MG90S servos sit inside the body, each turning a drum. Servo 1 pulls the bearing-1 collar round. Servo 2 pulls a free ring, and cords from that ring work bearing 2. Bearing 3 has no servo: a cord loop makes it mirror bearing 2.

Before you run it

A printed fan at 18,000 rpm can shed a blade. Run it behind a board, wear eye protection, and never stand in line with the fan disc.
